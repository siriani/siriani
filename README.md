## whoami

Ph.D. in Agribusiness and Development (UNESP), college professor, and Head of Research & Innovation at [CIAg](https://ciag.org.br). I build computer vision and deep learning systems for livestock monitoring — mainly multi-object tracking and behavior classification for laying hens — and lead applied R&D at the intersection of AI and agribusiness.

- 🔭 Currently finishing my dissertation: *Spatiotemporal Approaches and Dynamic Computer Vision Modeling for Laying Hen Behavior Classification*
- 🌱 Exploring agentic AI / small language models for agronomic decision support
- 💬 Ask me about multi-object tracking (YOLO, BoT-SORT), precision agriculture, or running ML training on a homelab instead of the cloud
- 📫 allan@fatecpompeia.edu.br
